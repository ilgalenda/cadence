Forms

A form in this platform is a set of decisions someone is making about work that will run. So the controls state the decision plainly — a checked box is filled charcoal, a selected segment inverts, a switch travels 2px and stops — and the form itself is one column at a readable measure, because scanning left and right across a settings grid is how people miss the thing they have just turned on.
