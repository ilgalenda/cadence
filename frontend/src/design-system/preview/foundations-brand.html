Brand marks

Two marks, and they are not interchangeable. Cadence is the platform: four bars at uneven heights, one of them blue — the beat the whole system is named for, and the same figure as the thinking indicator. Owl is the assistant inside it: a solid head with the eyes and beak knocked out to whatever surface it sits on. The platform signs the product; the owl signs an answer.

On charcoal the blue bar switches to --accent-on-chrome. Brand blue at 3.2:1 is legal as a shape on white but too dark to hold against the frame.

Clear space is the height of the shortest bar on every side. The wordmark is Roboto Regular at −0.03em; never Light, never all caps, never letter-spaced open.

Owl — the assistant mark

Owl
Owl

The knocked-out shapes take the colour of the surface behind the mark, not white — drop it on a coloured surface and the eyes must still read as holes. Owl never appears beside the Cadence wordmark; it identifies the assistant, not the product.

Not this

NeverRe-colour the bars into a gradient, or set more than one bar in blue. One bar is the beat; four blue bars are a chart.
NeverPut the Owl mark in a circle, a rounded tile or a speech bubble. It is a silhouette and needs the surface behind it.
NeverSet either wordmark in Kode Mono. The mono face is the machine's readout, not the brand's voice.
