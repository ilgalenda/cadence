Measure

Owl's answers are prose and must be typeset as prose. The reading column is capped at 42rem — roughly 68 characters, the length at which the eye tracks a line without effort.

Precision Time Protocol distributes time by measuring the delay between a master and a slave clock, then correcting for it. The accuracy you can achieve depends less on the protocol than on whether every device in the path is aware of it.

A switch that timestamps packets in hardware adds nanoseconds of uncertainty. One that does not can add hundreds of microseconds, and no amount of downstream correction will recover that.
