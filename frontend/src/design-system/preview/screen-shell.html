Every full surface is the same construction: a white field with a slim white rail beside it, separated by a single hairline. The rail carries navigation; the field carries the work. There is no frame, no inset sheet and no dark region — charcoal appears only as objects (the primary pill, the bulk bar, a toast). Rules

Always One hairline between rail and field. The two are the same white; the line is the whole structure, so it is never doubled with a shadow or a tint.
Always The Cadence mark appears once per surface, at the top of the rail. Never twice, never in the field.
Never A dark region. Charcoal is an object — a pill, a bar, a toast. If a whole pane is charcoal, it is wrong.
Never A boxed sheet. The field runs edge to edge; containers inside it are cards and wells, not a page-within-a-page.
